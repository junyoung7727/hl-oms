# 10: Simulation: venue margin model and margin invariants

**What to build:** The simulation world answers margin, venue-setting and account-mode queries from its own venue model: account value follows fills, price, fees and funding. New profiles run the margin rules under random interleavings of fills, dropped and delayed reports, and restarts, and new invariants are checked at every step. Existing profiles give the same run when the margin policy is off. Scenario rows: S3, S5, S8, S11, S14, S21, S23. Claims: V-MARGIN-12, V-SIM-1.

## Comments

- Spec review r1 (reviewer claude-opus-5-5, head 37c6330), 2026-11-16. Finding 8 (nit, correct): `existing_profiles_give_the_same_run_with_margin_off` did not say what it compares with. - Implementation (implementer claude-fable-5-2, branch `t10-simulation-margin-model`), 2026-11-16.
  - Built, venue model (`crates/oms-sim/src/venue.rs`, used by the simulation world and by the simvenue process): account value = cash + position x mark. A fill moves the cash by the price paid or received and by its fee, funding is taken from the cash (`charge_funding`), `set_mark` moves the position value. A margin report states every position with its value, margin (value / leverage, or a number the driver sets), margin mode, leverage and maximum leverage (40). The venue takes the reduce-only flag: it refuses a reduce-only order with no opposite position or larger than it, and a resting one trades only what still shrinks the position, then the venue cancels the rest.
  - Built, world (`crates/oms-sim/src/world.rs`): `Profile::margin` activates a margin policy and makes the world do what the node's reconciler does (margin query per poll with a rising number, venue setting and account mode while the core does not know them, the mark as reference price). Profiles `margin_calm` and `margin_lossy`. New actions: price move, funding, operator clear, margin-query outage, late margin answer, a venue that states no margin (MM buffer). Checks after every input: `leverage-gate`, `margin-fresh`, `reducing-cancel`, `reducing-accept`, `reducing-entry`, `reducing-release`, `stale-cancel`. A test can drive a world itself: `start`, `advance`, `submit`, `operator`, `restart`.
  - Built, simvenue process: its margin report carries the model's account value and positions; `ref_price` also sets the mark; `venue_setting` sets the model's setting. The control `account_value` now moves the cash so that the account is worth that value at that moment; fills, fees and price move it on from there. No existing process test needed a change.

  - Deviations and choices where the ticket is silent:
    - `profile_margin_calm_release_invariant`: `margin_calm` injects no restart (it is `calm`). The restart is covered by an exact sequence at the head of that test and by the random restarts of `margin_lossy`, where the same check runs (13 restarts while REDUCING in 40 seeds). The new day and the recovery come from the random runs of `margin_calm`, which start 12 s before a UTC midnight.
    - The margin clocks of the two profiles are one fiftieth of the contract's defaults (poll 100 ms, fresh 600 ms, stale cancel 1.2 s; the reference-price age limit is 600 ms too), because a run lasts about 25 virtual seconds.
    - The assertions "the profile reached the rule" need 40 seeds or more (the older profiles use 10): a midnight while REDUCING or a stale cancel that finds an order happens a few times in 40 runs.
    - The model's position margin is value / leverage and does not shrink with a loss (the model keeps no entry price). The MM buffer therefore trips in the simulation only while the venue is told to state a margin of zero (`margin_lossy`).
    - A reduce-only order larger than the opposite position is refused at placement (not trimmed). The simvenue process does not get the flag (its wire has no such field) and treats every order as plain.
    - `leverage-gate` computes exposure in the world from the core's books and reference price with the contract's formula. It shares the core's inputs, not its code.
    - `margin_lossy` has fewer link drops and restarts than `crashy`, and orders rest longer than in `lossy`; with the rates of `crashy` almost no plain order was accepted and the stale cancel found nothing to cancel.
