# 01: Hold table: reduce-only orders pass the partial holds

**What to build:** With a margin policy configured, a strategy can send a reduce-only order while the account is in resync, has an in-doubt order, or has an unacknowledged mismatch, and the OMS accepts it. Plain orders stay refused under every hold, and a reduce-only order stays refused under the holds that refuse everything. Without a margin policy nothing changes: every hold refuses every new order, reduce-only included, so a journal recorded without a policy re-decides to the same result. This is the prefactoring that makes a state "only reduce" possible, so it lands first and alone. It adds the optional margin policy section itself (the core values of the contract's 설정 table with their defaults) and reads only whether the section is present; no rule reads a value yet. The node's checks of the section (mainnet refused without it, bad values refused) stay in ticket 02. ADR-008 §7 is replaced by the amendment text in ADR-018, including its sentence on the margin policy. Scenario rows: S3, S10, S21, S22. Claim: V-MARGIN-1. Every test below except the no-policy one runs with a margin policy set.
